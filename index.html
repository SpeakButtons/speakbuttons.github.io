FAQ

Does SpeakButtons require internet?

No. Core speech synthesis runs fully offline for reliable access anywhere.

Can I customize boards for specific situations?

Yes. Create, rename, and organize boards or sets—tailor for home, travel, medical, or daily use. Export and import easily.

How does Jumbo Text Mode help?

It enlarges your typed text to full screen in high contrast, making messages visible from afar or in low-light conditions.

Is fine motor control needed?

No. Large, adjustable buttons accommodate limited dexterity. Add icons for quick visual recognition.
